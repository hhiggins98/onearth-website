The Story

I didn't just learn functional medicine marketing.
I lived inside it for five years.

OnEarth exists because I saw a gap nobody was filling properly. Functional medicine practitioners are among the most skilled health professionals working today — many of them transforming lives that conventional medicine couldn't help. And most of them are invisible online.

Generic marketing agencies don't understand the functional medicine patient. They don't understand root-cause medicine, or the specific fears and hopes of someone who's been dismissed by ten doctors before finding a practitioner who actually listens. I do — because I spent five years building for one of the best in the field.

That knowledge is what makes OnEarth different. Not the tools. Not the templates. The understanding.

I take on a maximum of three clients per city at any time. Each practice receives direct access to me — not an account manager, not a support ticket. Every piece of work that leaves OnEarth has been reviewed by me personally before it reaches you.
